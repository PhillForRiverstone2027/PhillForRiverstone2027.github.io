About the Campaign & Our Vision

The Riverstone electorate is undergoing one of the most rapid transformations in New South Wales. Suburbs across our region—including Schofields, Quakers Hill, Riverstone, Marsden Park, and Rouse Hill—are welcoming thousands of new residents and young families every year. While this growth brings incredible energy and cultural diversity to Western Sydney, it also presents significant challenges that require dedicated leadership.

Phill believes that a community should never grow faster than the infrastructure required to support it. For too long, local development has outpaced the delivery of critical public resources. This grassroots campaign is built upon a fundamental principle: our residents deserve proactive representation that anticipates the future rather than constantly reacting to problems after they arise.

We are fighting for a future where families spend less time stuck in traffic and more time together, where local schools have the capacity and modern facilities to educate our children close to home, and where small businesses can thrive within an accessible, well-planned regional economy. This is not just a political campaign; it is a collaborative effort to ensure our community remains a vibrant, safe, and liveable place for generations to come.

Our Core Policy Framework

A detailed look at the core priorities driving Phill’s vision for the Riverstone electorate.

1. Infrastructure, Roads, and Public Transport

Rapid residential housing must be matched by equal investments in transport infrastructure. Our policy focuses on easing congestion on major arterial links, upgrading local corridors to handle increased traffic volumes safely, and improving connection frequencies for commuters utilizing regional rail and metro networks. We advocate for smart urban planning that integrates pedestrian pathways and cycling routes, linking residential neighborhoods seamlessly with transport hubs and shopping precincts.

2. Education and School Capacity

With an influx of young families choosing Western Sydney as their home, local primary and secondary schools are facing unprecedented enrollment pressures. Phill is committed to campaigning for the continuous funding, expansion, and upgrading of local educational institutions. Our priority is to ensure that temporary solutions are replaced with permanent, state-of-the-art learning environments, reducing class sizes and guaranteeing that every child in the electorate has access to high-quality public education within their local catchment area.

3. Open Spaces, Recreation, and Environmental Preservation

High-density and suburban growth must be balanced with the preservation of green spaces, parks, and recreational facilities. Mental and physical well-being are directly linked to community access to open spaces. Our platform prioritizes the development of local sports grounds, modern playgrounds, and protected natural reserves. We believe in safeguarding our unique local environment while providing active spaces where local sports clubs, community groups, and families can gather and thrive.

4. Public Accountability and Community Consultation

Effective governance relies on transparent communication. Phill believes that representatives should remain directly accountable to the people who elected them. Our campaign proposes the establishment of regular, accessible community forums, mobile office sessions across all suburbs in the electorate, and direct digital feedback pipelines. Your insights, concerns, and ideas should directly inform parliamentary advocacy and legislative decisions, ending top-down decision-making and putting community voices first.

Get Involved in Our Grassroots Movement

A political campaign is only as strong as the people driving it. We rely entirely on the passion, dedication, and energy of local residents who want to see positive change across the Riverstone region. There are several vital ways you can join our efforts and ensure our message reaches every household:

• Grassroots Volunteering: Join our field teams for regular door-knocking initiatives and phone banking sessions. By engaging directly with residents in meaningful, one-on-one conversations about local issues, we can build a clear picture of what matters most to voters and ensure their concerns are represented.

• Community Outreach & Events: Help us coordinate, set up, and manage local community events, mobile listening posts, and family-friendly barbecues. These events provide crucial opportunities for neighbors to meet Phill in a relaxed setting and share their perspectives on the future of our suburbs.

• Visibility Campaigns: Help us increase campaign visibility across the electorate by hosting a "Phill for Riverstone" yard sign on your property, distributing informational materials at local train stations and transit hubs during peak commute times, or sharing our digital policy updates across social media platforms.

Support the Campaign Financially

Running a highly competitive political campaign requires material resources. Because this is an entirely grassroots movement, we do not rely on massive institutional backing—instead, we are funded by small contributions from everyday citizens who believe in our message. Every dollar raised stays within the electorate and is directly allocated toward increasing our outreach capabilities.

Your financial contributions directly fund the printing of physical materials, including policy flyers and voting guides distributed at community centers and railway stations. Donations also allow us to purchase essential campaign visibility gear like yard signs, high-vis volunteer shirts, and banners, alongside managing targeted digital communications to reach undecided voters across our growing suburbs.

Mandatory Legal Information (NSW Electoral Commission Compliance)

Before making a financial contribution to the campaign, please review the following strict legal guidelines set forth by the New South Wales Electoral Commission:

• Eligibility: Political donations within New South Wales can only be accepted from individuals who are registered on the electoral roll, or entities that meet strict state compliance criteria. You must provide your full name and residential address upon making a donation.

• Prohibited Donors: Under NSW law, political campaigns are strictly prohibited from accepting donations from property developers, tobacco industry entities, or liquor and gambling industry corporations, as well as close associates of these prohibited entities.
